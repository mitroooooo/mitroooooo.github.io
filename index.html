About

Dmitry Ivashchenko is a music composer and a restless explorer of new sounds.

He has worked on promo and trailer campaigns for studios including Universal, Sony, Disney, and A24 — and continues to bring his music and style to projects worldwide.

In 2026 he launched his solo career, experimenting with his capabilities across various musical directions. Don't hesitate to check it out in the SOLO section.
